Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I start scoring a match right away? Open the OpenMat Android app and tap Practice Mode to immediately run a regulation NFHS folkstyle match clock and scoreboard without any tournament setup or network connection.
  • How do I connect an external TV scoreboard? Connect your Android phone or tablet to an external 1080p HDMI/USB-C DisplayPort monitor. OpenMat automatically detects the secondary display and renders the spectator scoreboard while keeping operator controls on the phone screen.
  • How do I clear local tournament data? Clear storage or uninstall the app in Android Settings, or email admin@pureengineering.com for assistance.
