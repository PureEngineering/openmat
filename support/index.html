Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I start scoring a match? Open the OpenMat Android application and tap Practice Mode. The application starts a regulation NFHS folkstyle match clock and scoreboard without network setup.
  • How do I connect an external TV scoreboard? Connect the Android phone or tablet to a 1080p HDMI or USB-C DisplayPort monitor. OpenMat detects the secondary display and shows the spectator scoreboard while keeping operator controls on the phone.
  • How do I clear local tournament data? Clear application storage or uninstall OpenMat in Android Settings, or email admin@pureengineering.com.
