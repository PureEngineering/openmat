OpenMat Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026 · Publisher: Pure Engineering, LLC

OpenMat (package org.openmat.app.matside) is an offline-first scorekeeper and connected-display scoreboard for folkstyle wrestling tournaments. Pure Engineering, LLC develops and publishes this application.

1. Information We Collect

OpenMat does not require user account registration, cloud sign-in, or personal contact data. The application includes no third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-tracking SDKs.

  • Practice and Local Scorekeeping Data: OpenMat stores match timers, bout scores, period choices, and event journals locally in an on-device SQLite and CBOR journal.
  • Tournament Roster and Bracket Projections: Tournament officials enter athlete display names, school affiliations, weight classes, and bout scores. OpenMat signs and shares this data with enrolled scorekeepers and spectators over the venue network or relay.

2. Device Permissions

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): OpenMat uses the camera on-device to scan QR codes for mat enrollment and scorekeeper pairing. The application processes camera frames in memory and never records, stores, or transmits them.
  • Foreground Service and Notifications (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS): OpenMat runs a foreground service to maintain the match clock and venue session during display output.
  • Local Network / Internet: OpenMat synchronizes signed brackets and mat scores between the tournament host, matside scorekeepers, and spectator views.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Pure Engineering, LLC does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

The Android application stores all scorekeeping journals and tournament databases in local device storage. Clear the application storage in Android Settings or uninstall OpenMat to delete all local data. Contact admin@pureengineering.com for assistance.

5. Contact Us

Send privacy questions or data deletion requests to Pure Engineering, LLC at admin@pureengineering.com or visit the Support Page.
